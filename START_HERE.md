# Start here — how to contribute to ELION

Welcome. ELION is an open, international engineering community building peaceful
technologies for humanity. Wherever you are and whatever your experience, there's
a way in. Pick the path that fits what you're looking for.

---

## 🌱 "I want to learn and build"  (students & newcomers)

You don't need to be an expert. Start here:

1. Say hello in **Discussions → Introductions**.
2. Pick up an issue labeled **`good first issue`** — these are scoped for newcomers.
3. Read the [Contributing guide](https://github.com/ELION-Global/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md), fork, do the work, open a pull request.
4. Ask questions freely — in the issue or in Discussions. Asking is welcome, not a weakness.

## 🛠️ "I want a real challenge"  (experienced students, engineers, builders)

- Look for **`help wanted`**, **`type: research`**, and larger tasks.
- Propose improvements, take on a sub-area of a project, or help review others' work.
- Reviewing pull requests thoughtfully is a first-class contribution.

## 🔬 "I want to advise, lead, or bring research"  (professors, researchers, senior engineers)

ELION needs experienced people to shape it, not just to file tickets. You can:

- **Advise & mentor** — review contributions, guide students, raise the engineering bar.
- **Lead a research thread** — take ownership of a sub-area of SKYBRIDGE, LOVE, or a new
  direction; define its requirements and open questions.
- **Bring your own research or project** — see "Propose a project" below. If it fits
  ELION's mission and peaceful purpose, it can become a tracked effort you steward.
- **Grow into a Reviewer or Maintainer** — see [Governance](https://github.com/ELION-Global/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md).

If you'd like to get involved at this level, open a **Contributor interest** issue and say
so, or start a thread in **Discussions → Governance** — we'll find the right fit.

---

## 💡 Propose your own project, idea, or opinion

ELION is meant to grow from the people in it.

- **An idea or opinion?** → **Discussions → Ideas**. No commitment — just share it.
- **A concrete project or research direction?** → open a **"Propose a project"** issue.

Lightweight path: *idea → discussion → if it fits the mission and peaceful purpose, it
becomes a tracked effort with an owner and its own work.* Anyone can propose; good ideas
come from anywhere.

## How our work is organized

- **Projects** group related work — [SKYBRIDGE](https://github.com/ELION-Global/skybridge)
  and [LOVE](https://github.com/ELION-Global/love). Each project has its own repository,
  where its charter, tasks, and discussions live.
- **Issues** are units of work, labeled by type (`bug`, `task`, `research`, `docs`,
  `design`) and area, with difficulty markers (`good first issue`, `help wanted`).
- **The board** shows what's open, in progress, and in review.
- Significant requirements get stable IDs (e.g. `SKY-THM-042`) so work stays traceable —
  the beginning of a structured record that will one day help match people to the work
  that fits them best.

## What we hold to

Peaceful purpose. Scientific honesty — no fabricated claims. Engineering rigor. Dignity
and respect for every person, everywhere. We build to help people, and we say honestly
what is a concept, a study, and a result.

**One capable person, anywhere in the world, can move a serious engineering mission
forward. Welcome — let's build.**
