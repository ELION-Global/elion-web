# ELION Mission

ELION exists to bring people across the world together to research, design, and build peaceful technologies that improve human life.

Love is the underlying motivation.
Science provides understanding.
Engineering provides capability.
Technology provides the means.
Space is a major frontier.
Humanity is whom we serve.

ELION is international and open to people regardless of nationality, religion, ethnicity, language, or background. It is a global effort rooted in dignity, cooperation, and shared human flourishing. Participation is open to all who contribute constructively toward peaceful, useful, and honest technological progress.

ELION is not a sectarian institution, and it does not seek to impose any single religious or political ideology. The founder's personal Christian inspiration is acknowledged as part of the motivation and worldview behind the project, but ELION is explicitly committed to being a respectful, inclusive, and international effort that welcomes people of many backgrounds and beliefs.

The purpose of ELION is to unite human creativity and discipline in service of a better world. Through science, engineering, and cooperation, ELION seeks to create tools and systems that support life, safety, knowledge, access, and human dignity.
