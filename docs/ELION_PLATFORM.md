# ELION Platform

ELION is not merely a website. It is the future digital infrastructure through which people discover projects, join engineering teams, contribute technical work, manage projects, share knowledge, review designs, and eventually participate in funded development.

The public website is the visible front door of the broader ELION platform. It presents mission, vision, projects, community, and trust. The larger system is what enables ELION to scale from a public presence into a working engineering ecosystem.

## Platform modules

### Public Experience

- Public homepage and brand presence
- Mission and vision communication
- Project discovery and public activity
- Community onboarding

### ELION ID

- Contributor identity and profile system
- Verified skills and roles
- Reputation and contribution history
- Access and permissions framework

### Projects

- Project discovery and presentation
- Project stages and milestones
- Technical scope and governance
- Public and restricted project areas

### Teams

- Team formation and membership
- Roles and responsibilities
- Coordination and communication
- Collaboration across disciplines

### Roles

- Role definitions across engineering, research, governance, and operations
- Responsible ownership and accountability
- Team composition and capability mapping

### Engineering Workspaces

- Shared technical environments for projects
- Collaboration spaces for design and research
- Work tracking and progress visibility

### Requirements

- Requirement management
- Traceability from need to verification
- Change control and review

### CAD / Simulation

- Design artifacts
- Modeling workflows
- Simulation and analysis inputs and outputs

### Code

- Source repositories and review workflows
- Version control integration
- Quality and security checks

### Research

- Technical literature and notes
- Research planning and documentation
- Knowledge capture and synthesis

### Design Reviews

- Architectural and design review processes
- Feedback and approval workflows
- Decision logging

### Verification & Validation

- Test plans and execution records
- Technical evidence and acceptance criteria
- Quality and reliability review

### Knowledge Graph

- Linking disciplines, projects, people, and artifacts
- Shared understanding across the organization
- Discovery of related concepts and work

### AI Engineering Assistant

- Project-aware technical assistance
- Knowledge retrieval and synthesis
- Workflow support for engineering teams

### Governance

- Decision processes
- Policy and standards
- Role-based oversight and accountability

### Funding

- Project funding paths
- Sponsorship and grants
- Budget and resource planning

### Partnerships

- Universities
- Industry collaborators
- Research organizations
- International organizations

## Current vs future state

NOW: public website / homepage

LATER: the other systems and infrastructure described above

This distinction is important. ELION's current phase is a public-facing foundation and trust-building layer. A full engineering platform will come later as the ecosystem grows.
