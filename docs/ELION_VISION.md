# ELION Vision

ELION's vision is to build a global engineering culture that turns collective intelligence into practical, peaceful, life-improving technology.

## Near term

- Global engineering community
- Open projects
- Contributor platform
- Research collaboration

## Medium term

- Funded engineering programs
- Physical prototypes
- University and industry partnerships
- International teams

## Long term

- Advanced spacecraft
- Orbital infrastructure
- Humanitarian logistics
- Planetary infrastructure
- Technologies that help humanity cooperate beyond Earth

ELION is not limited to a single website, product, or launch timeline. The public homepage is only the beginning of a much larger system: a network of people, projects, tools, and institutions working together to advance engineering capability for humanity.

The long-term objective is not only technological ambition, but also civilizational cooperation: helping people work together across borders, disciplines, and generations to solve real problems at scale.
